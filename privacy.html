Privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026. This is the one page where we're not kidding.

The short version

WhereTF is a private, invite-only family app. It only shares your location after you turn sharing on, and you can turn it off at any time.

What the app collects

  • Your account: your name, email address, and password, plus your app settings.
  • Your location, while sharing is on: coordinates and related details your phone reports, such as time, speed, direction, altitude, accuracy, battery level, and motion status.
  • Background location: sharing continues when the app is closed or not in use, until you turn it off or log out.
  • Your phone: a random device ID and a push notification token.
  • Places and alerts: the geofences and notifications set up in the app, and the arrival and departure events they create.

Who can see it

  • People connected to your device in WhereTF can see its location, history, and related alerts.
  • The WhereTF administrator can see all accounts, devices, and location data.
  • Your location isn't sold or used for advertising.

Services involved

  • The WhereTF server stores the app's data.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications, so it handles device tokens and the content of those alerts.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website. Once the app is online, Cloudflare will also handle its login and network traffic.
  • Map providers supply map images, and they may see your network address and the map areas your device requests.
  • Apple or Google may process data as part of running your phone's operating system.

The app doesn't include advertising, analytics, or crash reporting tools. This website doesn't set its own cookies or include analytics scripts. Cloudflare may process basic request information to deliver the site and keep it secure.

How long it's kept

Location history is currently kept until an administrator deletes it. There's no automatic deletion schedule yet.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing: tap the Sharing button on the map. People may still see your last location and history.
  • Limit access: change location, notification, or battery permissions in your phone's settings.
  • See or delete your data: ask the WhereTF administrator to show you, correct, or delete your account and location history.

Children

Children may only use WhereTF with the involvement of a parent or guardian, who should explain how sharing works.

Questions

Contact the person who invited you to WhereTF. They're the administrator, or they know who is.
