What is this?

WhereTF is a private map for people who agreed to be on it. See where everyone is, get an alert when someone arrives or leaves a place, and look back at where they've been.

It's invite-only. If you can't get in, the family has spoken, or someone forgot to add your email.

Frequently yelled questions

WHAT DOES THE TF STAND FOR?

"The Family." Where The Family. Why, what did you think it stood for? Your mother is very disappointed.

IS THIS TRACKING ME?!

Only if you turn it on. Then yes, enthusiastically. That's the product. Nothing is shared until you tap the button and agree.

HOW DO I TURN IT OFF?

Tap the green Sharing button on the map. It's that easy, and nobody can stop you. People may still see your last location and when it was sent, so have your story ready.

WHO CAN SEE ME?

People you've been connected with, plus the admin who runs the thing. Not the whole internet. The internet has enough problems.

WILL IT KILL MY BATTERY?

It uses some, and we're still measuring how much. Your 47 open browser tabs remain the prime suspect.

Install on Android

  1. Ask your family admin for the private download link, because it isn't on this page.
  2. Open the file and allow the install if Android asks.
  3. Open WhereTF. Enter your email and the code we send you, then log in with your WhereTF account. Yes, that's two logins. Blame the f*cking internet.
  4. On the map, tap Not sharing, read the notice, then tap Share.
  5. When Android asks about location, choose Allow all the time. Otherwise, you go missing as soon as the app closes.
  6. Allow notifications and physical activity. If asked about battery use, choose Unrestricted.
